Asclepius

Asclepius is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, on their own computer. It is not offered to the public and nobody else can sign in to it.

With the owner's permission it uses Google's APIs to:

  • show the owner's Google Calendar on their home dashboard and in a daily summary, and add events they ask for;
  • read the owner's own bank-notification emails in Gmail to keep a personal spending log;
  • back up the owner's own files to their Google Drive and read the documents and spreadsheets they ask about.
