Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026. Asclepius home

Who uses it

Asclepius is a private tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

Only what the owner approves on Google's sign-in screen: calendar events, Gmail messages (read, label and send), Google Drive files, Google Docs and Sheets, and contacts (read only).

How the data is used

  • To show the owner's schedule, keep the owner's personal spending log from their own bank emails, back up the owner's own files, and answer the owner's own questions.
  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer. When the owner asks the assistant something, the relevant text may be sent to the AI service the owner uses to answer it.
  • Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Storage

Copies are stored on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own encrypted backups.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Asclepius's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for this app.
